I built an auction house where machines are the only bidders.

almoneda sells public-domain art per API call. An agent asks for an image, gets a 402, pays a cent or two over x402, and leaves with the artwork, its catalogue data, and the link that proves the license. Every settled payment lands on a public ledger, transaction and all.

155,771 works from the Met, Cleveland, the Art Institute of Chicago and the National Gallery of Art, searchable by text or by image. Humans can watch the floor live, or send a house agent to bid with test money so you can see the payment clear on-chain.

https://almoneda-app.vercel.app
